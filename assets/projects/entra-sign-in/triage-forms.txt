TRIAGE CHECKLIST
[ ] Record impact, intended task, app, resource, and client.
[ ] Capture the time and time zone. Convert to UTC for the handoff.
[ ] Record the visible error and request or correlation ID when available.
[ ] Match the interactive sign-in to the tenant, user, app or resource, and time.
[ ] Record the sign-in result, authentication steps, applied policies, and relevant device evidence.
[ ] Assign the next check to an owner. Verify the original task after remediation.

CONDITIONAL ACCESS WORKSHEET
Matched event and time in UTC
App and requested resource
Policy and mode
Per-policy result
Grant control and whether the policy requires one or all selected controls
Authentication and device evidence from the event
Conditions that explain why the policy applied
Unresolved evidence
Next check and owner
Recovery check

ESCALATION TEMPLATE

Reproduction steps and affected context

Checks completed and remaining gaps
